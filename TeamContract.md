## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* Use Discord as a communication for class project outside of class.

* Each teammate agrees to respond to messages within 36 hours.

* Need to notify team if not able to make it to lectures/tutorials or able to complete allocated work by the deadline. 

* Actively listening to each team members ideas, and giving everyone a chance to meaningfully contribute.

---

### Work Quality  

* Each Pull Request will be reviewed by at least two team members.
* Give regular updates to team members about progress of individual work.
* Notify team members immediately if you push changes to main branch.

---

## Decision Making

* Decsisions will be made by consensus when possible. If consensus cannot be reached, at vote will be taken; majority rules.  

---
## Conflict resolution

* By listening to each other's side of the issue and attempting to reach a compromise. By consulting a TA or instructor as a team, if a resolution has not yet been reached.
